## Design Context

### Users

YKSG employees, department leads, location managers, organization owners, and
limited support staff use this product throughout the workday. They need to
find the correct conversation quickly, coordinate accountable tasks, review
documents, accept meetings, and manage access without exposing patient health
information.

### Brand Personality

The interface is composed, reliable, and invisible. It must feel calm enough
for routine coordination, explicit enough for urgent operational work, and
precise enough for staffing and access administration.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a grounded, low-noise three-pane workspace that combines Zalo's shell with
Slack's channel model. Graphite + Indigo is the fixed theme. It uses
lower-luminance tinted surfaces, rare accent color, and quiet separators.
Reserve burnt orange for meaningful urgency. Avoid bright white expanses,
generic dashboard card grids, colored side stripes, glass effects, gradients,
and decorative motion.

### Design Principles

- Preserve context: location, channel scope, membership, and urgency remain
  visible where decisions are made.
- Standardize the shell: every employee and administrative route uses the same
  rail geometry, active states, icon sizing, and surface hierarchy.
- Reveal complexity progressively: routine messaging stays simple while tasks,
  documents, meetings, access rules, and administrative controls open when
  needed.
- Communicate with more than color: pair status colors with labels, icons, and
  explicit state text.
- Support clinic workstations and tablets with complete keyboard and touch
  interactions, WCAG 2.2 AA targets, and English/Vietnamese parity.
- Keep the product operational: warn before content that may contain patient
  information is sent and never present this frontend as a compliance control.
